About Umair

From weekend hobby edits to full-time storytelling — the journey behind the work.

The Journey

Turning passion into polished stories

I started my creative journey three years ago as a hobby — helping a friend edit his YouTube uploads on weekends. What began as simple cuts quickly grew into a deep love for storytelling through editing, sound design, and color grading.

Over time I expanded into graphic design and UI/UX design, realizing that the same principles that keep a viewer watching a video also make interfaces feel effortless and brands memorable. Eventually I turned it into a full-time career.

What makes me different is that I do not just cut clips together. I build a narrative that keeps the viewer watching until the very end — every transition, grade, and sound choice serves the story.

My mission is to help creators and brands turn raw, unorganized footage into polished stories that grow their audience.
